Management Takeaways

Evidence-based observations and suggested follow-up analyses

Category Management: Monitor Baby & Children and Home & Living closely, as their recorded net revenue is nearly identical despite different transaction volumes.

Assortment Analysis: Investigate the strong revenue concentration in washable baby diapers and coloring sets. Product-level margin and availability data would help evaluate commercial opportunities.

Customer Analytics: Segment repeat purchasers by order frequency and total spending. Consider RFM analysis when a larger customer history is available.
